Accommodation

Three rooms. One easy booking.

Twenty-two rooms across three types — every one air-conditioned, with a double bed, an attached bathroom with a geyser, an LED TV with cable channels, and daily housekeeping. Live rates for your dates open on our booking page.

The Classic Room

Everything you need and nothing in the way. A double bed with charging points beside it, a full wardrobe, a sofa chair and a desk that works as a desk — with an in-room telephone to reception when you want something brought up.

Eleven Classic rooms, including interconnecting options for families and friends travelling together. Ask when you book.
